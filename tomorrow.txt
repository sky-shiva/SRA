## ⚠️ Important Notes & Alternatives

### 📊 Dataset Considerations:
1. **IMOCHA Dataset Issue**:
   - The `imocha-ai-org/ssf-skill-extraction-pairs` dataset has known JSON parsing problems on Hugging Face
   - If skill extraction training fails due to this, you may need to:
     - Skip the IMOCHA dataset and rely on ESCO sentences + silver labels
     - Find alternative skill extraction datasets
     - Consider manual labeling for a small gold set

2. **Sample Sizes for Testing**:
   - Use `--max-samples-per-dataset N` flag during development to limit download size
   - Remove this flag for full production training
   - DAPT corpus size controlled by `--dapt-samples` parameter

3. **Free AI Generation Quality**:
   - The rule-based generator (synonym replacement, sentence shuffling, section reordering) creates realistic variations
   - Suitable for training authenticity detectors without API costs
   - Produces linguistic variations that mimic common AI generation patterns
